## Guards and acceptance gates

- **Draft and permissions:** The Manager launcher remains provisional only: save, reopen, edit and preview without final invoice issuance, payment, stock reservation/decrement, goods release, or posting on reconnect. Owner posting in local fixtures is an explicit simulation, after fresh review of changed terms. Preserve atomic effects, lost-response retry without duplication and rejection of conflicting reuse. Deny unapproved operations by default. Verify denied access at the data boundary and check staff outputs, nested/derived fields, exports, backups, caches and sync for restricted data, not merely hidden UI. Local fixture roles are not hosted authorization.
- **Legacy blocked (OPEN-001/008):** The repository HTML identity is confirmed; deployment dependencies and verified isolation are separate. Do not serve or launch the legacy HTML before the [inert browser gate](PHASE1_DISCOVERY.md#inert-browser-gate-not-run) passes with the required OS-level deny evidence and positive control. Keep all legacy runtime variants not run until then; run L01 first inside the verified environment.
- **Hosted and protected data blocked (OPEN-002/003/004/006):** D1 covers reversible synthetic local work only. Separate approval is required for protected metadata/records and isolated hosted testing. No live endpoints, credentials, real records or production writes under D1. Keep permission matrix and stale-term authority pending; require service-boundary and staff-device checks, individual accounts, account switching, cross-device sync, reconnect/revocation, concurrent confirmation and server atomicity. No unverified source precedence or material rule change without the required written decision.
- **Device and parity blocked (OPEN-005):** Capture local markup, file bytes and sink calls separately from actual clipboard, print, PDF, installed-app behavior and WhatsApp image handoff. Verify each inventory case against the frozen baseline on approved Windows, macOS and Android targets, with roles and expected versus observed evidence. A sink receipt, browser or emulator isn't acceptance; unresolved parity failures and untested targets don't pass.
- **Migration and cutover blocked (OPEN-002/003/007):** Separate authorization for protected rehearsal, provenance and idempotent import, record/control-total reconciliation and invoice continuity, including rollback and preservation of post-switch sales. Require owner/accounting sign-off, parity sign-off, a write pause, one writable billing path, rollback authority and distinct production cutover approval. Never reopen against a stale snapshot.

The [approved PRD](PRD.md#requirements), its [phase exits](PRD.md#phase-1---discovery-and-baseline), and the [owner checklist](OPEN.md#consolidated-owner-checklist) remain controlling. No phase exit or owner acceptance is claimed. At D handoff, record exact tested branches, build/commit, run IDs, expected versus observed results and artifacts in RESULTS, batch integrated and focused tests with the offline regression suite and Supervisor review, and retain failed, ignored, blocked and not-run outcomes separately. Optional demo feedback isn't a gate.
