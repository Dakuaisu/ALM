# Open items
## OPEN-001 Deployed baseline and dependencies
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must supply the deployed laptop copy or an independently computed digest and deployment details, including origin, browser/OS, assets and integration configuration evidence without secrets. Until then, preserve the supplied HTML unchanged and use it only as a discovery candidate; do not claim equivalence or complete dependency capture (DEC-012).
## OPEN-002 Live metadata and record access
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must provide protected Supabase metadata or explicitly authorize least-privilege read-only discovery access, plus approved Sheets and recurring JSON samples and a way to obtain protected snapshots. Do not contact live endpoints, infer deployed schema from client code, or assign source precedence (DEC-013).
## OPEN-003 Source precedence and invoice rules
- Status: OPEN
Trace the sources by record type, including Sheets write behavior and JSON snapshot scope. Verify invoice series, periods, GST treatment, stock and godown boundaries, and overlap. Bring conflicts and proposed precedence to the owner and accounting reviewer before migration.
## OPEN-004 Permission and draft conflict decisions
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must approve the role and data matrix, provisioned device rules, and who may resolve stale price, tax, stock, or credit terms. Discovery prepares the proposals; unapproved operations remain denied.
## OPEN-005 Targets and output handoff
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must confirm Windows and macOS laptop and Android tablet models and OS versions, and approve WhatsApp test accounts or recipients. Local image, print, PDF and simulated share are development only; they do not establish actual handoff or platform acceptance (DEC-014).
## OPEN-006 Hosted test project and implementation authorization
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must authorize the isolated hosted project and test access and separately approve implementation after Phase 1 evidence is available. Do not use production as a substitute.
## OPEN-007 Cutover and rollback authority
- Status: OWNER-BLOCKED
The owner must confirm the production write pause, identify the accounting signer and rollback authority, and separately authorize production cutover after rehearsal and parity sign-off. Confirm how post-switch sales will be retained and reconciled before any rollback.
