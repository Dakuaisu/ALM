## Planned fixture and evidence setup

Use the discovery isolation checklist before any future run. Freeze the seed revision, clock, time zone and browser build. Reset to the original seed before independent variants, especially edits, cancellations, deletions, restores and sync conflicts. For output branches capture the generated artifact or intercepted arguments, never send a message or invoke a real printer. For sync substitute in-process responses at the transport boundary; assert attempted calls and their order without contacting an endpoint. Stop if isolation cannot be verified.

- **E:** Empty disposable database, sync off.
- **B:** One named customer with opening due, a separate same-name customer with a distinct ID, and a walk-in. Seed active and cancelled sales around a month boundary, with the cancelled bill having the highest number. Use Cash, Online, unpaid and part-paid variants; put a separate dated receipt after an older bill. Use fractional prices, packet loading, a Piece item and a Case item with a known conversion, plus a crate containing both.
- **P:** Two suppliers with different rates for one matched product, another product with no supplier rate, an unmatched PO item and nonzero opening stock. Order five cases of the matched item; use two dated receipts at different prices, entering the later document date first. Include same-day lot variants, a shortage and a physical adjustment. Record stock in a common unit alongside case quantities.
- **T:** Synthetic business and party states, products with defaults and overrides, HSN and cess, one crate and fractional GST-inclusive values. No real tax or contact data.
- **X:** Disposable snapshot/revision variants, malformed import, distinct local and mock-remote data, fake device labels, intercepted output and scripted transport failures. No live configuration or accounts.

Record the case ID and variant, before/after collection and record IDs, affected derived totals and visible messages. Capture document date separately from creation time, raw amount separately from display rounding, and an artifact or digest for each output. Keep observations and verdicts outside this matrix.
